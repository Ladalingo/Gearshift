# Description
You are a pizza delivery driver and the pizzeria gave you the Bird Siesta Mk8.

Because the boss couldn't afford the maintenance your brakes suddenly fail!

You must still deliver the pizza or else you are fired! But you better not get fired, because if that happens, the employment agency is going to send an execution squad to your location!

Fortunately, the Siesta has a 500hp and 1000nm torque engine with a 5-Gear Manual Transmission. Use it to your advantage to slow down the vehicle.

But don't shift to aggressively and go above the redline, or else engine and transmission is dead and so do you.
