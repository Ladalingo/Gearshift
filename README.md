# Description
You are a pizza delivery driver and the pizzeria gave you the Bird Siesta Mk8.

Because the boss couldn't afford the car's maintenance, your brakes suddenly fail during your shift!

You must still deliver the pizza or else you are fired! You better not get fired or else the employment agency & tax office is going to send an execution squad to kill you!

Fortunately, the Siesta has a 500hp and 1000nm torque engine with a 5-Gear Manual Transmission. Use it to your advantage to slow down the vehicle.

But don't shift too aggressively and go above the redline, or else the engine and transmission is killed and so are you.
